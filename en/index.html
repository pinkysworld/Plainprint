Frequently asked questions

Open the full help page ↗
Will my printer work?

Plainprint works with network printers (Wi-Fi or LAN) that support AirPrint, IPP Everywhere or Mopria. The free compatibility check shows what your printer can do before you buy.

What about USB printers?

USB printers are not supported.

Do I need an account?

No. Plainprint does not require its own account and contains no ads. The app uses the Microsoft Store for purchase and licensing.
