Frequently asked questions

Are USB printers supported?

No. Plainprint works with network printers only (Wi-Fi or LAN). USB printers are not supported.

Which network printers work?

Printers that support AirPrint, IPP Everywhere or Mopria – when they report compatible capabilities. The free compatibility check in the app shows what your printer can do before you buy.

How do I find my printer's IP address?

The printer's display usually shows it under network or Wi-Fi settings, or you can print a network status report there. Alternatively, look for the printer in your router's device list. Enter the address in Plainprint under "Add printer by IP address" if "Find printers" does not discover it.

Why can't I scan?

Scanning works only on multifunction printers that support eSCL/AirScan. Older HP devices are supported through HP's web scan interface. Print-only devices without a scanner cannot scan.

Can I print from Word or my browser?

Plainprint does not install drivers. For Word, Excel or your browser to use the printer, it must be set up in Windows – the assistant in the app shows you how. Alternatively, save Word, Excel and web pages as PDF first and print that with Plainprint.

How do the trial and purchase work?

Printing and scanning are free for 7 days. Status, ink levels and checks stay free forever. After that you buy Plainprint once – no subscription, no account, no ads. Purchase and licensing go through the Microsoft Store (coming soon).
