Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Network, printer discovery and printing

Plainprint does not send usage telemetry or advertising/analytics events to Plainprint. Printer discovery, status checks, and printing use network connections to printers on your local network. This includes the IPP requests required by the printer and print files you select. Discovery sends local multicast/DNS-SD queries and, when needed, bounded IPP status requests on the local IPv4 network; discovery requests may be unencrypted before you select a printer. The app asks for consent before accepting an unencrypted printer connection (ipp:// or http://). Status data and later print contents sent over that connection have no transport encryption on the network. For ipps:// or https://, TLS certificate validation remains enabled; the app does not bypass certificate errors. The app can also open Windows printer settings and the selected printer's web page when you use those features.

Support and local storage

The support feature opens your email app with a prepared message to mip@gmx.biz and selected general diagnostic codes. You choose whether to add details and send it. Printer addresses and settings are stored locally. The clearable local history contains up to 20 print operations, including file paths, page counts, settings, status and timestamps; for multiple photos it stores their paths. A separate local counter records confirmed print operations for the optional review link. Trial timestamps are stored locally. Clipboard images, shared files and print data may be processed in temporary local files; the app attempts to delete them; for clipboard images, shared files and newly generated print spools it also retries after a later restart. Unleased print spools left by older versions are not automatically deleted. Deletion is not a secure overwrite and may fail while files are locked.

Scanning

Scanning uses eSCL (AirScan/Mopria Scan) to receive pages from the selected device over HTTP or HTTPS. Scanner detection currently tries unencrypted HTTP first; HTTP scan data has no transport encryption. Pages remain in app memory for preview and can be saved as PDF or JPG where you choose; you can cancel the save dialog. Plainprint does not upload scan contents to Plainprint.

Statistics, pictures and crash note

The local statistics.json stores total pages and jobs, monthly page counts for up to 24 months with recorded activity, and the last print time for statistics and maintenance hints. Clearing print history does not clear these statistics. Device-provided printer pictures are cached locally in PrinterIcons. On an unhandled error, last-crash.txt may store a timestamp and exception details, potentially including file paths or device information. These statistics, cached pictures and crash notes are not sent automatically.

Microsoft Store and other processing

If you obtain the app through Microsoft Store, it may communicate with Microsoft Store services to check trial and purchase entitlement. This is separate from the local printer connection. Microsoft handles Store and licensing data under its own privacy terms. Plainprint contains no ads and does not require a separate Plainprint account. Temporary local files may be created while printing. Windows, the Store, network components, and the printer may process diagnostic data under their own behavior; this notice does not control or make absolute promises about all processing on your device or network.
